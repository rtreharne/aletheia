This notice explains what personal data Aletheia handles, why, who it's shared with, how long it's kept, and your rights under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

2. Students

When you open Aletheia from your course, we receive and keep:

  • From your LMS: your name, an ID your LMS uses for you (not your student number, unless your LMS uses that), your role in the course, and the course and assignment names and IDs. If your LMS doesn't send a name, we use your email address as your name.
  • What you upload: your file, the text we extract from it, when you confirmed it was the right file, and the generative AI policy you agreed to, with the time.
  • Your check: the learning objectives and questions written from your work, your answers, how long each answer took, and your alignment scores.
  • The integrity log: each time, during the questions, you leave the page, switch to another window or come back; copy, cut, paste or right-click; and the result of any room-word checks.

Your instructors see all of this. You see your objectives, your scores, your answers with explanations, and how many times you left the page.

We use it only to run the check for your institution: to write your questions, show results to you and your instructors, and, where your LMS supports it, hand your file in to your course gradebook. Your institution decides the lawful basis. For universities this is usually public task (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(e)).

No automated decisions. Aletheia doesn't decide anything about you. It never sets or changes a grade, and it doesn't conclude whether you wrote your work. Your scores and integrity log are evidence that your instructors weigh using their own judgement.

3. Staff

For instructors and other course staff we receive your name, LMS ID and course role, and keep the assignment settings you choose, invitations you send to students to try again, and when you did so. We use this only to run Aletheia for your institution.

4. Website visitors and people who contact us

  • Contact form and calls: your name, email address, institution and message, and the time of any call you book. We use them to reply, to arrange the call, and, if your institution becomes a customer, to administer the contract. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and selling Aletheia (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Demos: your answers to demo questions, kept with a random ID, not your name.
  • IP addresses: kept for up to one hour to stop the contact form and demos being abused, then discarded. Our hosting provider may also keep short-lived server logs.
  • Cookies: we use only two cookies, both strictly necessary: a session cookie that keeps you signed in to the course you opened (or your demo), and a security cookie that protects forms. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and load nothing from third-party websites.

7. Security

Aletheia is only served over HTTPS. Students and staff reach it only through their LMS, which proves who they are with signed LTI 1.3 messages; there are no separate passwords. Students can see only their own work, and staff only their own courses. Files handed in to an LMS are fetched through signed links that expire.

8. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, and to have it corrected or deleted; to object to or restrict how it's used; and to receive it in a portable form. Some rights depend on the lawful basis. If you're a student or staff member, please contact your institution first: they decide on requests about course data, and we help them respond. Otherwise, contact us at {{ contact_email }}. We reply within one month.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

9. Changes

We'll update this notice if what we do changes, and show the new effective date at the top. We'll tell customers in advance about any new sub-processor.
