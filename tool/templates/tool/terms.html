2. Orders and the pilot

  1. An order covers one department for one academic year. It starts when we accept it in writing (email is enough) and ends on 31 August. It does not renew. To continue, place a new order.
  2. A free pilot covers one course, up to 300 students, for one semester, as agreed with you in writing. Pilots are free. At the end of the pilot you may place an order or stop. If you stop, you owe nothing.

3. Fees

  1. The fee is £5 per counted student, per academic year, plus VAT.
  2. A counted student is a student who hands in at least one attempt in the department's courses during the academic year. Each student counts once in that year, however many courses and assignments they use. Students who never hand in are not counted. Our records of hand-ins decide the count, and we will show you them on request.
  3. The minimum is 100 counted students (£500 plus VAT) per department, per academic year, whenever in the year the order starts.
  4. We invoice the minimum when we accept the order. After 31 August we invoice any counted students above 100, at £5 each. Invoices are payable within 30 days. We accept purchase orders; if you need a purchase order number on an invoice, give it to us before we invoice.
  5. There is no limit on attempts, other than the limits you set, and no charge for attempts beyond the fee for each counted student.
  6. If an invoice is unpaid 30 days after it is due, we may charge statutory interest under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, and suspend Aletheia for that department after giving you 14 days' notice.
  7. We may change our prices for future academic years. A change never applies to an order we have already accepted.

5. What Aletheia's results mean

  1. Aletheia uses an AI model (Claude, provided by Anthropic) to write learning objectives and questions from each submission. AI-written questions can occasionally be unclear or wrong.

  3. Aletheia never sets, changes or clears a grade. All marking decisions are yours.
